Last Updated: September 2026

Welcome to the Teacher Assistant app. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles your information. We do not collect, store, or sell any of your personal data on our servers.

Interacting with these external platforms is subject to their respective privacy policies. Messaging us directly via WhatsApp shares your phone number and profile info strictly for support purposes.
